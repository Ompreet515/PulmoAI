PulmoAI: AI-Powered Pneumonia Detection System

Hope you are doing well. PulmoAI is an AI-driven clinical decision-support platform designed to assist in early pneumonia detection using multimodal medical imaging.

“Combining intelligence, transparency, and trust in healthcare.”

About the System

PulmoAI integrates fine-tuned deep learning models with explainable AI techniques and an agentic AI interpretation layer. The system not only predicts pneumonia probability from Chest X-rays and CT scan slices, but also provides visual reasoning and clinically meaningful explanations, enabling interpretable and transparent AI-assisted diagnostics.

Core Capabilities

AI-Based Detection

Pneumonia prediction using fine-tuned convolutional neural networks optimized for medical imaging analysis.

Multimodal Intelligence

Fusion-based decision system combining Chest X-ray and CT scan information for robust clinical assessment.

Grad-CAM Interpretability

Visual attention mapping highlighting regions influencing model predictions, supporting diagnostic transparency.

SHAP Explainability

Pixel-level feature attribution revealing critical image patterns contributing to AI decision-making.

Agentic AI Interpretation

AI-generated clinical explanations translating model outputs into structured, medically meaningful insights.

Transparent Decisions

Designed to enhance clinical trust through interpretable predictions and explainable AI mechanisms.

Clinical Decision Support, Not Replacement

PulmoAI is designed as an assistive AI system to support medical analysis. Final diagnosis and treatment decisions should always be made by qualified healthcare professionals.
